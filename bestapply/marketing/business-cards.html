📄 What this is + how to print

Layout: 2 pages, 10 business cards per page, A4 size. Card size = 89 × 51 mm (standard NA business card).
Front (page 1): QR + brand + tagline "Check me before you apply". Back (page 2): value-prop side on dark navy.

To send to a print shop: Click 🖨️ → Destination: Save as PDF → Paper: A4 → Margins: None → Background graphics: ON → Save. Send the PDF plus these specs:
  • A4 paper, portrait
  • Duplex / double-sided, flip on long edge (default)
  • Weight: 300–350 gsm cardstock, matte or silk finish
  • Cut along the grey borders — 10 cards per sheet
  • Quantity: whatever you need. 100 cards = 10 sheets.
Since every card is identical, front/back alignment is automatic — no matter how the printer flips the paper, each card's back lands on its own front.
