Questions Canadians actually ask

Does my resume or profile ever leave my browser?

No. Your profile and job history live in your browser's local storage — never on our servers. Analysis happens by calling Claude directly from your browser with your own API key (BYOK), which means we can't see it even if we wanted to. When we launch the hosted plans, resume text is sent to Claude via a proxy, held for 24 hours for support, then deleted.

How is this different from just pasting a job into ChatGPT?

ChatGPT has read the whole internet — including US-first career blogs, US salary norms, and no clue what a PGWP is. BestApply is prompt-engineered on top of Claude with Canadian labour-market context baked in: NOC 2021 codes, Statistics Canada CMA cities, CRA tax brackets, and every Canadian work-permit category. The verdict factors in whether you can even legally take the job, not just your skills.

I'm on a work permit / studying / applying for PR — will this help me?

Yes — that's exactly who it's for. Tell it your status (PGWP, closed permit, student visa, applied under Express Entry, etc.) and the analysis will flag postings where the employer will need to sponsor you, LMIA-exempt roles you probably didn't know existed, and roles where your NOC code affects Express Entry points.

Is it available in French?

Oui. Choose Français from the answer language dropdown and every analysis comes back in French — verdict, strengths, gaps, action plan. Right now the UI is English-first; a French UI is on the roadmap for launch. If you're in Quebec and want the whole app in French, join the waitlist and reply "FR please" — it moves French UI up the queue.

Can I try it before paying anything?

Yes. Free tier gives you 3 full analyses — no credit card. Bring your own Anthropic API key and it becomes unlimited for free (BYOK mode). The paid plans exist for people who don't want to fiddle with API keys and want cover letters + interview prep.

When does the paid version launch?

We're taking the waitlist first so we don't build a checkout for zero traffic. Join the list, get 50% off launch pricing, and you'll be first when we open. Realistic ETA: 4–6 weeks after we hit 100 waitlist signups.

Does it work outside Canada?

The AI analysis works globally, but the reference data (NOC codes, CMA cities, CRA brackets, work-permit categories) is Canadian. If you're job-hunting in the US or Europe, ChatGPT is probably a fine choice — this one is deliberately narrow so it can be sharper on Canada.

Who built this and why?

Built by an immigrant to Canada who was tired of applying to jobs that never replied — and tired of watching friends do the same. If the market is going to auto-reject 98% of applications, at least you should know which 2% are worth your time.
