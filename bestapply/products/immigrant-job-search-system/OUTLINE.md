# The Immigrant's AI Job Search System

**Target audience:** H-1B, OPT, PGWP, BOWP, and PR-applicant job seekers in tech, finance, healthcare, and adjacent professional roles across North America (US + Canada).
**Price point:** $47 USD (~$65 CAD)
**Format:** ~60 page PDF, plus a downloadable ZIP of the templates and prompts as `.txt` / `.md` files
**Voice:** direct, immigrant-to-immigrant, zero corporate-speak, evidence-based ("here is the exact number, here is the exact fix")
**Anti-goal:** not a book on "how AI works" — this is a runbook on how to *deploy* AI to land offers under visa pressure

## Content sourcing / claims

Every number cited (250-to-1, 2 % reply, 15 % immigrant salary discount, 25–50 CRS points, 40 % LinkedIn hire origination) must have a citation footnote. Sources to lean on:
- IRCC Express Entry rounds
- Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey
- BLS Occupational Employment
- LinkedIn Talent Insights (public reports)
- USCIS H-1B data releases
- Academic papers on immigrant hiring bias (there's a solid 2019–2023 body of work)

**Do not invent numbers.** If a stat isn't sourceable, either omit it or replace with "our own analysis of N applications by [type of immigrant]" — reader tolerance for anecdote is high, tolerance for fake numbers is zero.
